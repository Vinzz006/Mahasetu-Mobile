# Brag Plan: MahaSetu Mobile Application

## What is this app?
MahaSetu Mobile is a secure government interoperability platform that empowers citizens to submit information once, grant verifiable consent, and track multi-department statutory processing in real time across 5 independent verification gates.

## The angle
"State-of-the-art national digital infrastructure: Submit Once. 5-Party Statutory Verification. Zero Document Duplication."
Eliminating the friction of physical queues and fragmented government departments by uniting Department of Revenue, Social Welfare, Labour, State Administration, and Compliance Auditing into a single, synchronized mobile gateway.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Government services shouldn't mean 5 separate queues."
Three fragmented department silos shrink and fuse together into a sleek, glowing digital crest: **MahaSetu Mobile**.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1 (4.0s - 8.5s)**: The Citizen's "Submit Once" Flow — Priya Sharma's dossier is verified once, encrypted with user-controlled consent toggles, completely eliminating duplicate document submissions.
- **Moment 2 (8.5s - 14.5s)**: The 5/5 Statutory Matrix in Real Time — Live verification cards for:
  1. `DEPT_A` — Revenue & Civil Supplies [APPROVED ✓]
  2. `DEPT_B` — Social Welfare & Inclusion [APPROVED ✓]
  3. `DEPT_C` — Labour & Employment Welfare [APPROVED ✓]
  4. `ADMIN` — State Administrator Oversight [APPROVED ✓]
  5. `AUDITOR` — Statutory Compliance Gate [APPROVED ✓]
  Watch the counter race from 0/5 to 5/5, unlocking the official `APPLICATION_VERIFIED` badge with an emerald shimmer.
- **Moment 3 (14.5s - 17.5s)**: Multi-Role Architecture — Instant switching between Citizen, Dept Officers, Admin, and Auditor with strict role-based access control and Twilio server-side security.

## Outro / punchline
"One platform. 5-party trust. Real-time governance in the palm of your hand."
MahaSetu official seal settles center stage with platform release metadata.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Citizen logs in, views unified digital identity, and provides cryptographic consent.
2. **Action**: Real-time multi-department inspection kicks off across Dept A, Dept B, Dept C, State Admin, and Compliance Auditor.
3. **Result**: 5/5 multi-personnel verification completes with immutable tamper-evident audit status: `APPLICATION_VERIFIED`.
